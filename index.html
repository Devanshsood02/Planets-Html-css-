Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet in the Solar System and the closest to the Sun, named for the Roman messenger god due to its speed as it orbits the Sun in just 88 Earth days. It has a cratered, Moon-like surface and an enormous, iron-rich core that makes it very dense. Because of its thin, non-existent atmosphere, Mercury experiences extreme temperature swings, reaching hundreds of degrees Fahrenheit during the day and dropping far below zero at night.
